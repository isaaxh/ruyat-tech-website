How we work

Six stages, no surprises. You know what's happening at every one.

Not sure where you are? Start a project and we'll help you find the right first step.

  1. R/01

    Discovery & planning

    We learn about your business and goals, then agree on the features, timeline and scope before any work begins.

  2. R/02

    Design & prototyping

    We design the key screens and turn them into a clickable prototype you can try and approve.

  3. R/03

    Development & testing

    We build your product in small steps and test it on real devices along the way.

  4. R/04

    Iteration & feedback

    We share progress with you regularly, take your feedback and make changes early.

  5. R/05

    Deployment & launch

    We publish your product under your own accounts and check that everything works live.

  6. R/06

    Maintenance & support

    After launch, we keep your product secure and up to date, fix issues and add improvements.

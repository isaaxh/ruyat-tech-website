Construction

Built on a regular hexagon.

All geometry sits on a 100-unit grid. Use the master SVG files and never redraw the mark.

Hexagon

Pointy-top regular hexagon, circumradius 45, centred at 50,50. Width 78 × height 90.

Eye

Two quadratic curves from 22,50 to 78,50 with control points at 50,24 and 50,76. Aperture 56 × 26.

Pupil

Square rotated 45°, diagonal 25.46, centred at 50,50.

Fill rule

Even-odd: one single-colour shape, so it reproduces cleanly in one ink.

Section 06

Motion

Soft, easy, unhurried.

Ruyat TechBrand Guidelinesv1.0 — 202606 · Motion
R/3921.4225°N · 39.8262°E+
Motion principle

Easy flow, never a performance.

Motion on our website should feel like breathing: gentle, continuous and almost unnoticed. Elements drift into place and settle softly. Nothing snaps, bounces, spins or shouts. If a visitor notices the animation before the content, it is too much.

01

Soft arrivals

Fade up a short distance (20 px) and settle with a long ease-out. Elements arrive, they never pop.

02

One flow at a time

Stagger siblings by 70 ms so the page reads top to bottom like one gesture. Never animate everything at once.

03

Calm in the background

Ambient motion (the lattice) is slow, low contrast and keeps away from text. It adds atmosphere, not attention.

04

Quick, quiet feedback

Hover and press respond in 160 ms with small changes: a 2 px lift, a gold glow, an underline that slides.

◆ Do

Opacity and small translate · long ease-out · stagger · gentle glow · one focal motion per view

✕ Avoid

Bounce or elastic easing · spin and flip · parallax-heavy scroll-jacking · fast flashing · motion over body text

Ruyat TechBrand Guidelinesv1.0 — 202606 · Motion
R/4021.4225°N · 39.8262°E+
Timing & easing

Fast to respond, slow to settle.

One easing curve does most of the work: it starts quickly and decelerates for a long, soft landing. Durations stay short for feedback and generous for reveals.

instant 100 msColour and focus changes
fast 160 msHover, press, toggles
base 280 msMenus, tabs, cards
slow 600 msScroll reveals, page load
ambient 8 sLattice beam and loops
Stagger 70 ms between siblings, up to 6 · Reveal distance 20 px
Ruyat TechBrand Guidelinesv1.0 — 202606 · Motion
R/4121.4225°N · 39.8262°E+
Facet lattice in motion

Three ways the grid comes alive.

Always behind content, always slow. Use one per section, and vary them across the site: a flash on the hero, a beam between sections, a pulse for a calm spotlight. Never stack more than two.

01 · flash

Tile flash

Random tiles light up in gold and fade out like a slow heartbeat. In 400 ms, out 1.2 s. Max 4 tiles at once, 0.6–1.2 s apart, peak fill 14%.

02 · beam

Edge beam

A soft comet travels only along the connected hexagon edges, turning at corners. Slow: 60 px/s with a 320 px tail. Variation drift: two fainter beams, 40 px/s, 460 px tail.

03 · pulse

Pulsating edge light

A light source breathes behind the grid, a 7 s swell and fade. Tiles stay dark; light shows through the edges only, brightest near the source, and follows it as it drifts slowly.

SafetyPauses off-screen and in hidden tabs
TextDims to 25–35% behind headlines and cards
Reduced motionStatic lattice, no flash, beam or pulse
Codemode: 'flash' | 'beam' | 'pulse' | 'all'
